Overview of RefDiT-VSR. The framework fuses LR video features with adaptively gated noise features, concatenates the resulting video tokens with arbitrary-reference tokens, and performs one-step super-resolution using a fixed-timestep DiT. (a) The LR-conditioned noise gate predicts an input-dependent noise weight α. (b) Reference-and-Neighbor Attention aggregates reference and temporally adjacent tokens within each DiT block. Flame and snowflake icons denote trainable and frozen modules, respectively.
